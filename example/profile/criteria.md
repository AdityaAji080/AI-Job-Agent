## The shape of the role

Machine learning and backend engineering roles that ship to production.
Research engineering counts. Analytics-only work does not.

## Seniority

Years of experience are not a filter; record them in the experience column. Skip only if the listing hard-requires a PhD.

## Markets and work authorization

In priority order (the run's slots are split by `MARKET_SHARES`):
1. Singapore — needs an Employment Pass from the employer.
2. India and Canada, equally — no sponsorship needed.
3. United States — needs employer paperwork; skip if the listing requires citizenship.

## Skip only for these

- A PhD as a hard requirement
- Internships
- Analytics, BI, sales, or operations work
- Staffing agencies hiring for an unnamed client
- Closed listings, or postings more than 90 days old with no future date named
- No link that reaches an application

## Where to look

LLM tooling, model evaluation, applied AI, computer vision in production.
