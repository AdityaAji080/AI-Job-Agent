## The shape of the role

Machine learning and backend engineering roles that ship to production.
Research engineering counts. Analytics-only work does not.

## Seniority

Skip only if the listing states a minimum of 7+ years or hard-requires a PhD.

## Markets and work authorization

In priority order:
1. India — no sponsorship needed.
2. Canada — no sponsorship needed.
3. Singapore — needs an Employment Pass from the employer.
4. United States — needs employer paperwork; skip if the listing requires citizenship.

## Skip only for these

- 7+ years required, or a PhD as a hard requirement
- Internships
- Analytics, BI, sales, or operations work
- Staffing agencies hiring for an unnamed client
- Closed listings, or postings more than 60 days old with no future date named
- No link that reaches an application

## Where to look

LLM tooling, model evaluation, applied AI, computer vision in production.

## Limit

At most 25 new jobs per run.
